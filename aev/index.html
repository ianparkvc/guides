The Problem
A $300 billion market
built on a lie

The Blend Trap

Pre-workouts, multivitamins, greens powders — they all promise everything in one scoop. But “proprietary blend” is code for “we won’t tell you how much.” You’re paying for a mystery box.

Stuck in the 90s

Powders need shakers and water. Capsules feel like medicine. Even gummies are big, sticky, and come in bulky bottles. Nothing fits in your pocket. Nothing fits in your life.

Broken Economics

DTC brands spend 40-60% of revenue on customer acquisition. The brands that win aren’t the ones with the best product — they’re the ones that can outspend on ads. That’s an arbitrage machine, not a health company.

Red Ocean

Create Wellness opened the creatine gummy category. Now everyone has piled in — Optimum Nutrition, Bounce, Bear Balanced, Legion, Swoly, ESN. The gummy format is already commoditizing.

The next winner won’t be another creatine gummy brand.
It will be something different entirely.

The Insight
We’ve seen this movie before

Apple cider vinegar was one of the most validated natural health products in the world. People knew it worked. But drinking it was disgusting. Then Goli put it in a gummy and built a $430M revenue brand in two years.

Our CSO Ian Park was on the Goli investment team at VMG Partners (which invested $20M). We saw firsthand what happens when you change the form factor of a proven ingredient. You don’t need to prove the science. You just need to make it easy.

The battle has shifted from efficacy to experience. The question isn’t “does it work?” It’s “how do I want to take it?”

Gummies were the first answer. But gummies have their own problems:

  1. They get sticky. Heat, humidity, time — gummies degrade.
  2. They’re heavy and large to carry. Big bottles don’t fit in your pocket.
  3. They aren’t clean to eat. Residue on fingers. Not discreet.

Now think about Tic Tac. Easy to carry. Fun to eat. Clean to use. What if supplements felt like that?

Four core insights
Insight 01

Deconstruct, don’t blend

Serious athletes already stack single ingredients. The problem is it’s only available in powder tubs. What if stacking was as easy as popping a Tic Tac?

Insight 02

The form factor revolution is barely started

Gummies, gum, beadlets, films, tabs — nobody has combined nano form factor with supplement-grade dosing in a format you enjoy eating. That’s the gap.

Insight 03

East meets West

Traditional Asian medicine formulas have never been made accessible in modern formats. Our CEO co-founded one of the top 4 Chinese medical schools in the US. K-wellness is the next K-beauty.

Insight 04

Own the marketing engine

The brands that win will crack AI-native marketing — creative at scale, real-time optimization, automated influencer ops. We’re building that engine. Aev is the first brand to prove it works.
