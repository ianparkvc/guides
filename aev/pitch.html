The Product
Nano candy in a tin case

Each piece is a micro functional candy. Not a big gummy you chew through. Not a horse pill you force down. A tiny candy you pop without thinking — like a Tic Tac that happens to be creatine. Five pieces a day. Five seconds. Less effort than opening a water bottle.

Single ingredient per tin. Transparent dosing. No blends. No fillers. No guessing. Your stack is however many tins you carry.
